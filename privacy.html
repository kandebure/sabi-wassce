Sabi Public Beta Privacy Notice

Beta version: 0.9.0. This static beta stores the student's profile, practice history and optional feedback in the browser's local storage on that device.

What this beta stores

First name or nickname, selected WAEC country, exam year, daily study target, completed sitting history and feedback the user chooses to enter.

What this beta does not require

No email address, phone number, payment details or real-name account is required.

Control

Users can export or delete their locally stored data from the Profile/footer controls. Clearing browser site data will also remove it.

Important limitation

This package has no production server or cloud account system. If it is later connected to analytics, authentication or a database, this notice must be replaced with a production privacy policy before collection begins.
