Micro lesson

Shifts vs movements

A change in the good's own price causes a movement along the demand curve. A non-price determinant such as income, tastes or the price of a substitute can shift the curve.

Exam trap: do not say “demand increases” when you mean “quantity demanded increases.”

Best launch wedge: combine the strongest patterns from premium exam platforms — syllabus notes, target tests, smart learning, wrong-answer repair, mock builders, planners, skills and analytics — but make the intelligence specifically WASSCE. Start with Sierra Leone + a small set of high-demand subjects, prove that students return and improve, then expand country coverage and school licensing.
