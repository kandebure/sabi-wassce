Sabi Public Beta Terms

Sabi is an independent revision prototype and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the West African Examinations Council (WAEC).

The beta uses original exam-style practice questions for testing the product experience. It does not claim that its sample grade bands, readiness estimates or advice are official WAEC predictions.

Students should use official WAEC instructions and their schools for current examination rules, syllabuses and entry requirements.

Question content should be reviewed by practising subject teachers before the beta is promoted as an authoritative academic resource.
